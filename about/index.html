Our story · Ladakh

Hi, I am Rafail.

I am a young entrepreneur from the beautiful land of Ladakh, driven by a passion for learning, creating, and building something of my own.

  1. 1

    The challenge

    Many people saw my physical disability as a limitation, but I chose to see it as a challenge to overcome. With determination and belief in myself, I started my own YouTube channel and stepped into the world of business at a young age.

  2. 2

    The work

    My business focuses on natural, high-quality products, from pure and healthy peanut butter to refreshing Multani Mitti (Fuller’s Earth) face masks. Every product reflects my commitment to quality, care, and customer satisfaction.

  3. 3

    The climb

    My journey has not always been easy, but every challenge has made me stronger, and every customer has motivated me to keep moving forward. As a young entrepreneur from Ladakh, I aspire to grow my business, explore new opportunities, and inspire others to believe in themselves.

What we bring you

Rafail Natural brings the produce of Ladakh, the high-altitude desert in the Himalayas, to homes across India. At over 3,500 metres, with clean air, glacier water and long sunny days, Ladakh grows fruits and herbs that are rich in flavour and nutrition.

  • Seabuckthorn: the “wonder berry” of the Himalayas, rich in vitamin C and omega oils.
  • Apricots: sun-dried Ladakhi apricots, kernels and apricot oil.
  • Apples: crisp mountain apples and homemade jams.
  • Traditional foods & teas: roasted barley flour (tsampa), herbal teas and more.

Our promise

We buy directly from farmers and women's cooperatives in Ladakh, pack carefully, and ship all over India. Our products are natural, with no artificial colours or preservatives.
