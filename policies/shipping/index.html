Shipping policy

Last updated: 2026

We deliver to all serviceable pincodes across India.

Shipping charges

  • Orders of ₹1,000 or more: free shipping.
  • Orders below ₹1,000: a flat shipping charge of ₹80.

Delivery time

Orders are packed and dispatched within 1–3 working days from Leh, Ladakh. Delivery usually takes 4–9 working days depending on your location. Remote areas and weather conditions in Ladakh may cause occasional delays.

Tracking

Once your order ships, we will email you the courier name and tracking number. You can also check your order status on our Track Order page.
